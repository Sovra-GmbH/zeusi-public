What Zeusi is

Zeusi is a shared memory for your AI assistants. Your assistant connects to it as an MCP connector at https://mcp.zeusi.dev/mcp. You sign in with an existing GitHub account and get your own workspace. It starts empty.

Your content

You decide what goes in your workspace. Zeusi writes into it only after you approve a preview, or through a sync routine that you set up yourself. Never put passwords, access tokens, sign-in codes or private client details in Zeusi.

You own your content. You give Sovra GmbH a licence to store and process it and to serve it back to you, only to run Zeusi.

What Zeusi’s answers are

Every item keeps its source. Zeusi never makes up a decision. A Zeusi decision is context, not permission: your assistant still needs your current, explicit approval for anything that needs one. A connector cannot force a model to call a tool or run a script. Your assistant decides whether to follow Zeusi.
